Keyboard: WASD to move, QE to turn, Space to stop. Release to stop, commands expire automatically.

Dance

Idle

Tap a move and Spot performs it for about 7 seconds, then settles. Tap another mid-routine to switch to it immediately.

Basic moves

Give Spot a couple of metres of clear space, dancing lifts feet, rotates the body and hops.
