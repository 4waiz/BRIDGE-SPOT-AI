Dance moves

Idle

Tap a move to perform it. Tapping another mid-routine switches to it immediately, the new sequence preempts the old one on the robot.

Basic moves

Postures and gaits rather than dances, useful for getting Spot back to a neutral pose between routines.

How it works

No licence needed

Boston Dynamics' own choreography service needs a paid licence this robot does not have. These moves avoid it entirely, each one is scripted here from the two commands any Spot accepts: a body pose and a velocity.

Motion, not poses

Every move is a function of time, evaluated twenty times a second and streamed to the robot. Driving it continuously rather than stepping between fixed poses is what makes it read as dancing.

In place or moving

Moves marked stays in place only articulate the body - the feet stay planted, so the robot cannot walk into anything. Those marked moves around drive the legs and need room.

Before you press play

Give Spot a couple of metres of clear space and keep the tablet controller within reach. Pressing another move mid-routine switches immediately; the routine ends by returning to a neutral stand.
